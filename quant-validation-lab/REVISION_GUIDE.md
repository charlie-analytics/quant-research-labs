# Revision guide

Edit lab.py and objective synthetic fixtures in test_lab.py. Run tests before a fresh named output directory. Freeze lookback, horizon, ranking choice, training boundary, costs and universe before evaluation. Do not overwrite earlier runs. The command and schema are in README.md. Costs must change arithmetic without changing selection. Purge training labels unavailable at evaluation start. Keep matched benchmark clocks and reject incomplete paths.

Reused history remains retrospective. Resolve original source provenance and suspicious observations before claiming data validation. A large cached result alone proves neither authenticity nor corruption. Retain before/after results for corrections. Website deployment configuration and raw history are deliberately excluded.
